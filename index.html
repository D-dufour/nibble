Grilled Chicken & Mango Salad

Fresh, bright, layered.

Ingredients

Chicken
  • chicken breasts
  • olive oil
  • lime, juiced
  • garlic cloves, crushed
  • smoked paprika
  • ground ginger
  • Salt & black pepper
Salad
  • ripe mangoes
  • avocados
  • large red pepper
  • medium carrots
  • mixed salad leaves
  • spring onions
  • fresh coriander
  • fresh mint
  • roasted cashews or peanuts
Dressing
  • olive oil
  • limes, juiced
  • honey
  • gluten-free tamari
  • garlic clove
  • fresh ginger, grated
  • fresh chilli, optional

Method

  1. Marinate. Mix the chicken marinade ingredients, coat the breasts and leave for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Dress. Whisk olive oil, lime, honey, tamari, garlic, ginger and chilli until glossy.
  3. Build. Combine leaves, mango, avocado, pepper, carrot, spring onion, coriander and mint.
  4. Grill. Cook chicken over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes per side, until the centre reaches 75°C. Rest 5 minutes.
  5. Finish. Toss the salad gently with most of the dressing, top with sliced chicken and nuts, then drizzle the rest over.
Plating

Use a wide shallow bowl. Keep the chicken centred and slightly elevated, with mango and avocado visible around the edges. Finish with herbs, cashews and a final ribbon of dressing.

Lamb Chops & Mint Sauce

Restaurant-style, without the fuss.

Ingredients

  • lamb chops
  • baby potatoes
  • baby carrots
  • green beans
  • fresh mint
  • rosemary
  • thyme
  • garlic cloves
  • shallots
  • lemons
  • red wine
  • gluten-free beef/lamb stock
  • red-wine vinegar
  • balsamic vinegar
  • tomato purée
  • honey
  • caster sugar
  • olive oil
  • ground cumin
  • smoked paprika
  • Salt & black pepper

Method

  1. Potatoes. Roast halved baby potatoes at 210°C with olive oil, garlic and rosemary for 30–40 minutes.
  2. Carrots. Roast with honey, oil and thyme for 20–25 minutes until glazed.
  3. Beans. Blanch 3–4 minutes, then toss briefly in olive oil with seasoning.
  4. Mint sauce. Dissolve caster sugar in a little boiling water, add red-wine vinegar, lemon and finely chopped mint.
  5. Jus. Soften shallot and garlic, cook tomato purée, add red wine and herbs, reduce, add stock and reduce again. Finish with balsamic and honey; strain.
  6. Lamb. Season with garlic, herbs, cumin, paprika, lemon and oil. Sear 2½–3½ minutes per side, render the fat edge, then rest 5 minutes.
Plating

Set three chops in a loose fan, potatoes to one side, carrots and beans in neat bundles, then spoon jus around the meat. Keep the mint sauce in small accents rather than covering the crust.

Duck, Sweet Potato & Cranberry

Rich, crisp, balanced.

Ingredients

  • skin-on duck breasts
  • sweet potatoes
  • baby carrots
  • fresh/frozen cranberries
  • oranges
  • garlic cloves
  • thyme
  • rosemary
  • red wine
  • gluten-free chicken stock
  • brown sugar
  • cinnamon stick
  • honey
  • olive oil
  • Salt & black pepper

Method

  1. Sweet potato. Peel, cube and boil until soft. Drain very well and mash with olive oil, salt and pepper until silky.
  2. Cranberry-orange sauce. Simmer cranberries with orange juice/zest, brown sugar, cinnamon and a splash of red wine until the berries burst and the sauce thickens.
  3. Carrots. Roast at 200°C with honey, olive oil and thyme for 20–25 minutes.
  4. Duck. Score the skin. Start skin-side down in a cold pan, then cook over medium heat 8–10 minutes to render the fat. Turn and cook 3–5 minutes more. Rest 8–10 minutes.
  5. Jus. Deglaze with red wine, add stock, rosemary and thyme, then reduce until glossy.
Plating

Swipe sweet potato across the plate, fan the duck slices over the edge of it, arrange carrots opposite and dot cranberry sauce around the plate. Keep the crisp skin exposed.
